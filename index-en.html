Club Introduction

Kangwon National University Central Game Development Club Potanity
POTATO + UNITY

Potanity is the central game development club at Kangwon National University,
where students from various fields such as game planning, programming, graphics, and sound gather
to create creative and innovative games together.

We are honing our skills through projects using Unity and
various activities such as regular studies, regular meetings, planning studies, participation in events like game exhibitions, and game jams.
Anyone passionate about game development is welcome!

Regular Activities

  • ⏰ Regular Activity Hours: Weekdays 6 PM - 9 PM
  • 📚 Regular Study & Monthly Pizza Day Meetings: Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays
  • 🤝 Autonomous Meetings: Wednesdays, Fridays
  • 💡 Other Activities:
    • 📝 Planning Study
    • 🎮 Minigame development practice after study completion, team projects, medium-scale projects during vacation
    • 🕹️ Visiting game exhibitions, attending game shows, internal club game jams
    • 🍕 Every first Wednesday and third Friday of the month is Pizza Day!

Potanity Minigame Introduction

Discover minigames developed by Potanity!

Minigame 1: Metal Slug

An action shooting game paying homage to the classic masterpiece Metal Slug. Clear various weapons and stages to defeat enemies!

Minigame 2: Reaction Speed Test Game

When a signal appears on the screen, click as quickly as possible to measure your reaction speed! Challenge your reflexes.

Minigame 3: Watermelon Game (In Development)

A puzzle game where you stack fruits to make a watermelon! Strategically stack fruits to aim for the highest score.

Minigame 4: Pico Park (In Development)

A multiplayer puzzle game where cooperation is key! Work together with friends to clear stages.

Minigame 5: Motion Recognition (In Development)

A motion recognition game using a webcam! Move your body to complete various missions.

Minigame 6: Sorting Game (In Development)

A game where you quickly sort given objects into the correct categories! Test your agility and concentration.

Minigame 7: Mars Escape (In Development)

A survival game where you survive in a hot desert! Manage limited resources and survive as long as possible.
